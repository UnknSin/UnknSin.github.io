Privacy policy

This app is a private rclone configuration used by a small group of invited people to synchronize game server files through Google Drive.

The app accesses Google Drive only on the computer of the person who signs in, and only to copy files between that computer and Google Drive. No data is collected, stored, sold or shared with the app owner or any third party.

Each user can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
